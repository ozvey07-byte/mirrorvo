What the service does and what it needs

MirrorVO lets you mirror your iPhone screen to compatible TVs on the same local network. Your phone and your TV must be on the same Wi-Fi network for it to work.

The app operates together with third-party devices and your home network. Your TV's capabilities, your router and how busy your network is directly affect mirroring quality, and none of these are under our control. A delay of a few seconds is inherent in this technology.
