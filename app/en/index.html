Three steps

How it works

  1. 01

    Same Wi-Fi

    Put your phone and your TV on the same network.

  2. 02

    Pick your TV

    Choose it from the list, or take the browser path.

  3. 03

    Start Broadcast

    The picture can take 10-15 seconds to appear on the TV.

Only you can start it. iOS does not let apps begin screen broadcasting on their own — the system prompt needs your confirmation every time.

The honest part

What it cannot do

These are on our App Store page too. We would rather you knew before downloading, not after.

Netflix, Disney+
They appear black. That is the publisher's copy restriction, not a missing feature — no app can get around it.
Latency
It is not instant. Expect a short delay; that is the nature of this technology.
Miracast
The "Screen Mirroring" option in your TV's menu is Miracast. iPhone does not support Miracast, and no app can use it.
AirPlay
Apple does not let apps start AirPlay mirroring directly. On AirPlay TVs, use Screen Mirroring from Control Center.
